# Your AI Agent Just Ran DELETE Without WHERE. Now What?

A speaking session by **Jonathan Stewart** · [sqlbites.net](https://sqlbites.net)

## Abstract

This session considers what to do when an AI agent runs a DELETE statement without a WHERE clause, including assessing the impact and thinking through a response. <!-- OWNER: confirm -->

## Audience

Database practitioners and developers working with AI agents that can execute SQL. <!-- OWNER: confirm -->

## Learning objectives

- Explain the risk of a DELETE statement without a WHERE clause. <!-- OWNER: confirm -->
- Identify questions to ask when assessing the impact of an unintended deletion. <!-- OWNER: confirm -->
- Outline a response to an unintended deletion initiated by an AI agent. <!-- OWNER: confirm -->
- Discuss safeguards for agent-initiated database changes. <!-- OWNER: confirm -->
