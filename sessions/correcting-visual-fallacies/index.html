Now you see it, but did you really?

Correcting visual fallacies · a session by Jonathan Stewart · sqlbites.net

Charts mislead — sometimes by accident, sometimes by design. This session walks the visual fallacies that quietly distort what an audience takes away (truncated and dual axes, misleading area and 3D, cherry-picked ranges, colour that lies) and shows how to correct each one so your visuals tell the truth, not just a story.
