# From Legacy to Modern: Your Data Platform Migration Strategy Guide

A speaking session by **Jonathan Stewart** · [sqlbites.net](https://sqlbites.net)

A practical playbook for moving a legacy data platform to a modern one on
Microsoft Fabric: assess what you actually have, choose a target architecture,
and migrate in phases instead of betting the business on a big-bang rewrite.
The sequencing decisions, quick wins, and traps that decide whether a migration
lands or stalls.
